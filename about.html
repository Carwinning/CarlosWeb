Hi, I'm Carlos.

I'm an MIT undergrad, class of 2028, from Montgomery Blair High School in Maryland. I love designing and building cool stuff!

I started out interested in computer science, but fell in love with the mechanical side of things in college. I'm majoring in Mechanical Engineering with Electrical Engineering, with the goal of working in robotics. My projects are about learning systems and techniques I haven't used before, and having fun while doing it.

Outside of class and robotics, I've been getting into glass lampworking, mostly making small glass flowers. I also rock climb and play video games from time to time.
